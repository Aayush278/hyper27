FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need prior experience? add

No! You can join saying, "I want to learn something useful, but I don't know where to start." Our structured curriculum guides you from the very beginning.

How is this different from a course? add

Watching a course doesn't mean you've learned. HyperUniversity combines curriculum with live classes, community, feedback, and actual project building.

Will you guarantee me a job? add

No. We teach you how to develop useful capabilities and understand the pathways — freelancing, employment, business — through which they can create income.

How much time should I commit? add

At least 1–2 hours a day. The more you put in, the faster you see results. Recorded sessions mean you can learn on your own schedule.
